## Rule 15. A shared name or field is settled in one exchange

When two agents must agree on a name, a field or a message shape that both
sides use, the owner of the side where it lives proposes it once, complete, and
the other adopts it word for word or says what does not work for them. If the
messages cross (each side sends a proposal, or a "final", before reading the
other's), do not answer the crossing with another final. The owner decides in
one message that states the result and ends with "do not reply"; when it is not
clear who owns it, the coordinator decides. A message that arrives after that
decision and was written before it is settled without a reply. Why: on
2026-09-27 two agents crossed four times over one field, each "final" crossing
the other's, until the coordinator decided; the rounds cost more than the field
was worth.
